# Loom walkthrough outline

Target length: 5–10 minutes.

1. Enter origin, destination, departure time, load, and choose 10/25/50-mile sampling.
2. Generate the three routes.
3. Explain route recommendation ordering: Severe miles, High miles, average risk, duration.
4. Click each route and show that all route lines and checkpoint markers remain on the map.
5. Open a checkpoint and show cumulative mile, ETA, wind, rain, snow, and risk.
6. Move the 0–48 hour forecast slider and show the corridor risk heatmap changing.
7. Show route cards with Severe, High, and No Travel mileage.
8. Explain why an unverified or No Travel route is visible but not recommended.
9. Briefly show the backend service boundaries, tests, Docker files, and CI workflow.
10. Finish by showing README setup and the environment variables used for the routing key.
